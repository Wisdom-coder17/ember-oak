OUR STORY · SINCE 2018

A little fire.
A lot of heart.

We set out to create more than a restaurant. We wanted a place where people could slow down, connect, and eat exceptionally well.

HOW IT BEGAN

Rooted in the
simple things.

Ember & Oak began with a simple idea: bring honest ingredients, open-fire cooking, and warm hospitality to one table. Our kitchen celebrates the rhythm of the seasons and the character of the people who grow, catch, and make our food.

Today, that same spirit guides every plate and every welcome. We keep things thoughtful, never fussy, and always made to be shared.
